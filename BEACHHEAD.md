# Commercial Beachhead

Purpose: turn the existing HappyHomarid audience/content surface into a measurable standalone commerce beachhead without importing MTG or unrelated silo data.

MVP target: one audience problem, one concrete paid offer, one doorway, one fulfillment contract, one external payment proof.

Worker loop: discover demand -> prepare offer -> approval -> publish -> measure -> improve.

Revenue is only counted from independently verified payment evidence.
